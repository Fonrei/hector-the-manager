    Preset Manager

    Collect presets from .syx files or from the synth, arrange the Active Bank (the 32 programs of the synth) and keep everything else in the Library. Nothing is written to the synth until you use a Send button or an External-synthesizer preview.

    Pattern Manager

    Patterns are kept in the Library of this browser. The Synth Pattern Bank maps them onto the synth’s 16 pattern slots; nothing reaches the synth until you click a Send button. Bytes the app does not interpret are preserved untouched.

    How to organize the collection

    Double-click a Library preset number to assign it to an Active Bank slot (1–32). If that slot is occupied, its preset returns to the Library. If the slot is empty, subsequent numbers shift down.

    Double-click the name to rename it (8 ASCII characters maximum). Uppercase and lowercase letters are both allowed.

    Drag presets with the mouse: you can reorder them within the Active Bank or within the Library, but not move them between zones. Use “Link to Active Bank” and “Unlink” to change zones.

    To select multiple presets: Ctrl/Cmd-click, or Shift-click to select a range. Link, Unlink and Delete apply to the entire selection.

    Pattern ↔ preset link (PATTERN PROG LINK on the synth)

    Linked mode is ON: each slot shows whether its pattern carries link data received from the synth. A pattern created in this app has none, so the synth applies its default link to it. Sending patterns may make the synth recall the program linked to its current pattern.

    ON: selecting or receiving a pattern also recalls the program linked to it, so the sound can change. OFF: the pattern plays with whatever preset is selected. Set it to OFF if you choose the preset from the sequencer.

    16-Step Sequencer

    Active steps play in order. Inactive steps are skipped and take no time, so they shorten the loop. An active step with no notes is a rest: it lasts one step and plays nothing. Up to four notes per step.

    Per-step CC automationoff

    Four independent tracks. On each active step, every enabled track sends its value for that step (External synthesizer engine only).

    Sequencer LFOsoff

    Two LFOs that move a CC continuously while the sequencer plays on the synth. Set the speed with the slider, as a tempo division (follows the BPM), in Hz or in ms. An LFO can also modulate the other one: its rate (FM) or its depth (AM); when it does, its speed can go up to 1 kHz, the usual ceiling for audio-rate LFOs. Two LFOs cannot modulate each other at the same time. The “Synth’s own LFOs” group targets the Pro VS Mini’s internal LFOs (rate CC 72/73, amount CC 70/28).
